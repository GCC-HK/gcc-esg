1. Purpose and scope

The calculator produces an indicative estimate of the annual EU CBAM certificate cost. In default mode it now reproduces the official default-values scenario — the cost an EU importer faces if the supplier provides no verified emissions data. In custom mode the user's verified installation values are used instead. It is a screening tool, not a CBAM declaration; a prominent disclaimer states this above every result.

2. Calculation method, step by step

Inputs: product sector s, origin country c, import year y (2026–2034), volume V (t), emissions mode (official default or verified custom value E), aluminium product form where relevant, and optionally the carbon price actually paid at origin.

1. Default emissions (default mode): E = AnnexI(c, s) × (1 + markup(y, s))  — AnnexI values EXCLUDE mark-up
2. Benchmark: B = IR2620_ColumnB(bucket) [+ aluminium form add-on]; in default mode the bucket follows the route Annex I assigns to the origin country
3. Free-allocation deduction: FA = B × factor(y)  (factor per Dir. 2003/87/EC Art. 10a(1a))
4. Liable emissions: L = max(0, E − FA)
5. Carbon-price credit: r = min(1, Ppaid / PCBAM)  (Ppaid = user input, else headline table)
6. Cost: C = L × (1 − r) × PCBAM; annual total = C × V
7. Projection: steps 1–6 repeated for every year 2026–2034 (mark-up and factor change per year); ±€20 price sensitivity band displayed

5. Known limitations (displayed or documented)

  • Product granularity: one representative CN code per bucket (HRC for steel, unwrought for aluminium, grey Portland for cement, 3102 10 19 for urea). Other CN codes in the same category can differ; the full parsed Annex I (12,949 rows) is retained for future per-CN lookup.
  • Precursor cascades and Annex IV (precursors of unknown origin) are not modelled — that is the verified-data path by design.
  • Certificate price held constant in the projection (±€20 band shown); real ETS prices will move.
  • Art. 9 headline prices are approximations unless the user enters the effectively-paid price.

Regulatory watch: (1) IR 2025/2620 recital 10 — the 2026 benchmarks are based on estimated ETS 2026–2030 benchmarks and will be reviewed once the final ETS benchmarks are published; updated values would apply to imports from 1 Jan 2027. (2) The July 2026 ETS-review proposal would stretch free allocation to 2037/38; the projection follows current law and is footnoted accordingly on the page.

7. Maintenance rules

  • Certificate price: update on each Commission publication (quarterly 2026, weekly 2027+); the page labels the vintage.
  • Benchmarks: re-check when the Commission publishes final ETS benchmarks (expected before 2027 imports; recital 10).
  • Default values: re-extract on any further correction of IR 2025/2621 (subscribe to OJ alerts for the CBAM implementing acts).
  • All constants live in one data module (v2-cbam-data.js) with a review date shown on the page; every change requires a source and a green regression run (worked examples above are pinned).
