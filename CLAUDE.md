# ESG Sourcing Hub — GCC ESG Committee Platform

EU sustainability compliance platform by the **GCC ESG Committee** (German Chamber of Commerce, Hong Kong). Audience: Hong Kong sourcing offices of German/European retailers and their manufacturers in China/Vietnam. **V2 (hub + subpages) IS the site since 2026-09-05** — the V1 one-pager was retired by committee decision ("we do not need V1 anymore"; it lives in git history before commit "V2 replaces V1"). The Sep 2026 committee review round (50 Webvizio comments by Jill Dessel, REWE Far East) is implemented; her decisions govern current copy (requirements-not-rules terminology, no sign-in, member area "launching soon", CBAM calculator gated pending committee verification, VI parked, EU+DE+UK markets only). Review round 2 (6 comments, 2026-09-08, separate Webvizio project) is implemented, including the rename to ESG Sourcing Hub (owner decision 2026-09-09 after Jill's EmpCo concern about "green"; her "strictly forbidden" claim is legally overstated for a B2B platform name, the rename is an optics call). Open from round 2: a handful of her suggested certifications parked with questions back (ISO 32210/32220, LEED/BREEAM/EDGE, RJC, SCS, ISCC EU). Her nav label idea "EU Requirements" was rejected (site covers UK/DE law too; suppliers know "Regulations").

**Naming rules:** it is the "ESG Committee" — never "Working Group" (old name). The regulation checker is the "ESG Sourcing Compass" — never "Compliance Atlas" (belongs to the owner's other companies). The CBAM tool is the **"CBAM Quick Check"** — never "CBAM Calculator" (renamed 2026-09-19: the Chamber gives orientation, not calculation services; member companies own professional CBAM work). In V2 navigation, use plain names (Regulations, News, Deadlines, Guides, Find Support, About); the Compass product name appears only as its page masthead. The briefing page masthead is the plain title "Latest news about the EU regulatory landscape" (owner decision 2026-09-09, Jill's wording); "The ESG Sourcing Briefing" survives only in the subscribe email/product family. **ESG Sourcing Hub** is the platform brand (naming history: GREEN FUTURE → "Green Sourcing Hub" 2026-09-05 → ESG Sourcing Hub 2026-09-09, after Jill's EmpCo objection to "green"; the earlier "SustainAbility" idea was dropped over the ERM trademark collision — never reuse "green" or "sustainability" in the brand). It completes the product family: ESG Sourcing Hub (platform) · ESG Sourcing Compass (checker) · The ESG Sourcing Briefing (news). Positioning: "a Committee platform, not the Chamber's official website" (footer states this; no AHK/GIC logo without Chamber marketing sign-off).

Legacy detail pages kept in V1 styling: `regulation.html?id=<regId>` (old links only), `article.html?slug=<slug>` (also via `/article` rewrite with per-article OG tags), `privacy.html`; `account.html` exists but is unlinked (sign-in removed from nav).

## CBAM engine (V2) — data discipline

All constants live in `v2-cbam-data.js`, each with an OJ source comment: country default values from **IR 2025/2621 Annex I as corrected by IR (EU) 2026/1740** (base values excl. mark-up; mark-up 10/20/30% by year, fertilisers 1%; the Annex's route letter selects the benchmark; missing countries use the Annex's own "Other Countries" row), benchmarks from **IR 2025/2620 Column B**, factor schedule from **Dir. 2003/87/EC Art. 10a(1a)**. Full parsed corrected Annex I (12,949 rows): `scripts/seeds/cbam-annex1-corrected-full.tsv`. Maintenance: certificate price quarterly (weekly from 2027); benchmarks to be re-checked when the Commission finalizes ETS benchmarks (recital 10, before 2027 imports); re-extract on further Annex corrections. **Methodology document:** edit `scripts/cbam-methodology-source.html`, then `python3 scripts/build-cbam-docx.py` → `CBAM Calculator - Methodology and Verification.docx` (v3.1, for committee verification).

## Matchmaking (Find Support / Our Partners) — data discipline

The private workbook `input/GCC-Members-ESG-Mapping.xlsx` (gitignored, never commit/deploy/send to Grok) is the authoritative member-mapping source. `python3 scripts/build-matchmaking-data.py` regenerates `v2-matchmaking-data.js` (frontend dataset) + `api/match-taxonomy.json` (public taxonomy for the Grok classifier). Public dataset rules: only **ESG Provider = Yes** companies with High/Medium confidence; only name, website, offering, categories, keywords. "Partial" providers and all internal columns (Confidence, adopter/partner ratings, review notes, Strategic Partners/Top Adopters sheets) stay private — verify-v2 asserts no leakage. UX rule (owner 2026-09-19): results render only via the "Show solutions" button (topic and/or free text; picked topic leads), never on select change; ?category= deep links still render on arrival. Owner-decided data additions go INTO the workbook via idempotent scripts in `scripts/seeds/` (pattern: add-documentation-category.py, which added the 19th category "Technical documentation and product information" with Impala Services + Pergamon Labs), then regenerate. **When the owner replaces the workbook, earlier seeded additions are wiped — re-run the `scripts/seeds/*.py` workbook seeds before regenerating** (learned 2026-09-21: a refresh dropped the documentation category; also ran polish-offerings-sep21.py because new rows carried internal research-note language in the public Offering column). Find Support results render as a 5-column table (Company / Topics, matched categories only / What they offer / Website / Contact), stacked cards below 760px; the Our Partners directory keeps cards. Positioning is fixed: the Chamber connects and introduces (neutral, alphabetical listings, "not an endorsement" disclaimers, no partnership claims — the Strategic Partners sheet is a prospect list, not actual partners); member companies deliver the professional services. Introductions go via `info@hongkong.ahk.de`. New taxonomy categories in the workbook fail the generator until ZH/DE/VI labels are added in the script.

## Content rules (the Chamber's credibility depends on these)

- **Every factual claim verified against official sources** (EUR-Lex, Commission, gov.uk, law-firm briefings) before seeding. "Proposed", not "law", for pending files. Set `lastReviewed`. Full-content audit ran 2026-08-11 (~90% verified correct; corrections seeded via `seed-corrections-aug26.js`).
- Regulation matching is declarative (`categories`/`roles`/`sizes`, empty = all; `markets`). Reasons may contain `{category}`/`{role}` placeholders. `badge: 'new'|'updated'` + `newSince`.
- News format: what happened → why it matters → what to do, with `sources`; license-safe images only. Access tiers `public`/`registered`/`premium`; member guides use `pillar: 'guide'`.
- No invented case studies, contacts or claims. **The committee has no own email — use `info@hongkong.ahk.de`** (a `gcc-sustainability@…` address never existed).
